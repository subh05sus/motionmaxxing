# Roadmap

Built in phases. Each lands tested and is pushed to `main` on its own.

| # | Phase | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Repo | Licences, notices, docs, the vendoring tool. |
| 1 | The cut, German first | Transcribe on the Mac's GPU (whisper.cpp, large-v3-turbo), find every retake, false start, filler and dead pause, keep the last good take of each line (the video is watched where the takes are close), check the cut by listening to it again, learn how tight to cut from a raw take and its posted reel. |
| 2 | The studio | A Mac-style app (dark, Apple glass on floating controls): New video, Videos, the editor (video, transcript, notes, chat, timeline), Styles, Library, Settings, a guided first run. Claude runs behind it on your login; its questions appear as cards in the page. |
| 3 | The demo recorder | Claude drives a website or claude.ai in its own browser, records every state sharp, speeds up the waiting, follows the action with the camera, blurs personal data. |
| 4 | The styled edit | Captions, face zooms, real captures with the line marked, X and Reddit posts, quote clips, logos, motion graphics per beat in four placements (behind the speaker, half screen, a box, full screen), b-roll from the library and free stock, sound. |
| 5 | Export | Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve (FCPXML), Premiere (XML), EDL, SRT, a CapCut package, and a handoff folder for an editor. |
| 6 | Extras | Shorts from a long video, a product video from a URL, a reel copied onto your b-roll, creator teardowns, fact-checking a script. |

Later: cloud renders and a hosted studio.
